EXTEND `benchmarks/bmr_recovery.py` (created in 31-01) with two helpers:

1. `select_tempering_factor(true_vals, samples_fn, *, target=0.95,
   band=(0.90, 0.98), candidates=(1, 2, 5, 10, 20, 50, 100)) -> dict`
   - `samples_fn(T) -> torch.Tensor` returns posterior A samples drawn under
     tempered covariance `T * Cp` (the caller wires this to
     `temper_vl_posterior` + a multivariate-normal draw).
   - For each candidate T (ascending), compute empirical 95% coverage via
     `benchmarks.metrics.compute_coverage_from_samples(true_vals, samples_fn(T),
     ci_level=0.95)`. Return the SMALLEST T whose coverage enters `band`, plus
     the full `{T: coverage}` trace and the chosen coverage. If none enter the
     band, return the T with coverage closest to `target` and flag
     `in_band=False` (do not raise — surface it; research §C2).
   - Docstring: coverage-matching is the calibration the phase brief asks for;
     the band [0.90, 0.98] is a CHOICE reported as exploratory, not a validated
     schedule (research Open Question 3).

2. `tempered_vs_untempered_ranking(posterior_mean, posterior_cov, prior_mean,
   prior_cov, prunable_indices, tempering_factor) -> dict`
   - Untempered: `rank_connections(posterior_mean, posterior_cov, ...)`.
   - Tempered: `cov_t = temper_vl_posterior(posterior_cov, tempering_factor)`
     (THE PD guard — do NOT hand-roll a Cholesky), then
     `rank_connections(posterior_mean, cov_t, ...)`.
   - Return `{"tempering_factor": T, "untempered": <rank dict>,
     "tempered": <rank dict>}` for side-by-side reporting.
