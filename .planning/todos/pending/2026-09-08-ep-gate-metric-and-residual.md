## Two separate problems, do not conflate them

### 1. The metric is ill-posed on near-zero parameters

```
A_true    = [[-0.5,     0.15   ], [0.1,      -0.5   ]]
vl_A_free = [[ 0.00057, 0.14913], [0.10089,   0.00188]]
spm_Ep_A  = [[-0.06741, 0.07281], [0.17001,   0.05071]]
element_errors = [[1.0085, 1.0481], [0.4066, 0.9629]]
```

The A_free **diagonal** is a log-deviation from the fixed -0.5 self-connection,
so its correct value is ~0 and both engines report ~0. Dividing by an SPM value
of -0.0674 makes the relative error 1.0085 -- an artifact of the denominator,
not a disagreement. Two of the four elements (both diagonals) are pure noise in
this metric and they drag `mean_relative_error` to 0.8565.

Fix the metric before reading anything into the number: use absolute error, or
an error normalised by the prior SD, or restrict the gate to the free
off-diagonal entries the problem actually identifies.

### 2. The off-diagonals genuinely disagree -- and VL is the closer one

| entry | truth | VL | SPM | VL err | SPM err |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A[0,1] | 0.15 | **0.14913** | 0.07281 | **0.6%** | 51.5% |
| A[1,0] | 0.10 | **0.10089** | 0.17001 | **0.9%** | 70.0% |

VL also attains the **higher free energy** (517.88 vs 515.98) -- a better
optimum by SPM's own objective. The most likely reading is that SPM's
Gauss-Newton stopped short on this problem, not that VL is wrong.

**Caveat, stated plainly:** this is one seed on one N=2 problem, and the data
were generated by our own simulator. That the fitted model is exactly the
generating model favours neither engine a priori (the forwards agree to 4e-14),
but "VL recovers ground truth better than SPM" is NOT established from n=1.
